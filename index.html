Before you get in touch.

Can you help with smaller electrical jobs?

Yes. We install lights and outlets and track down electrical problems, as well as handling larger installations.

Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger?

Not always. We’ll check your electrical service and the charger you want. Load management may let you add charging without upgrading the service.

What should I include in my request?

Your town, how to reach you, and a short description of the project or problem are a useful starting point.
