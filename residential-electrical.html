Electrical work around the house

We install, repair, and replace home electrical equipment in Ann Arbor and Washtenaw County. You can start with a short description. You don't need to know the name of the part or diagnose the problem.

Lights and ceiling fans

We install recessed lights, light fixtures, under-cabinet lights, dimmers, and ceiling fans. Tell us whether you're replacing something or adding it in a new spot.

For a new location, access above the ceiling can affect the wiring work. For a fan, we check that the electrical box and support can carry it.

Outlets and switches

We replace damaged outlets and switches, add outlets where you need them, and install suitable GFCI protection. We can also check whether USB outlets suit your setup.

Tell us where the outlet is and what you want to plug into it. Some equipment needs its own circuit.

Hot tubs, spas, and saunas

We install the electrical connections for hot tubs, spas, and saunas. Give us the equipment model and planned location so we can check its power needs and the wiring route.

Larger installations

We also install EV chargers, generators, and electrical panels. We check your existing system before recommending changes.

Something stopped working?

A little detail helps us prepare. Tell us when it started, which rooms or outlets it affects, and whether anything changed just before it happened.

An outlet has no power

Let us know whether nearby outlets still work and whether a wall switch normally controls it. If a breaker or GFCI has tripped, tell us. Don't keep resetting a device that trips again.

A breaker keeps tripping

Tell us what you were using when it tripped. An overloaded circuit, damaged appliance, wiring fault, or breaker problem can cause it. We'll check the cause before recommending a repair.

Lights flicker or dim

Does it happen at one light or throughout the house? Does an appliance start at the same time? Those details help us work out where to look. Flickering alone doesn't prove you need a new panel.

What will the work cost?

Replacing a fixture where wiring already exists takes different work from adding lights across a finished ceiling. For repairs, we need to find the fault before we can price the full job.

Send your request and we'll follow up. We may be able to discuss the work by phone. If we need to see it, we'll arrange a visit and explain the next step.

Wiring in an older home

An older house doesn't automatically need a complete rewire. We check the wiring, previous alterations, and what you want to add.

Knob-and-tube wiring

We check its condition and any changes around it, including insulation and later wiring. If replacement makes sense, we can discuss whether the work can proceed in sections.

Aluminum branch-circuit wiring

Some older aluminum wiring needs specific repair methods at its connections. We inspect the system and explain suitable repairs or replacement. Ordinary wire connectors aren't a substitute for a method approved for that wiring.

Two-prong outlets

A three-prong replacement alone doesn't add a ground. We check the circuit before choosing a repair. In some cases, the code permits GFCI protection with the required labels, but that still doesn't create a grounding connection.

Small upgrades that solve everyday problems

  • Better lighting: Put light where you need it and choose compatible dimmers.
  • More convenient outlets: Stop relying on extension cords for permanent use.
  • Surge protection: Reduce damage from certain power surges with suitable protection.

If you notice heat, burning, or shocks

Stop using affected equipment and keep people away. Don't remove covers or touch damaged wiring. If you see fire or smoke, leave the area and call 911. For other urgent electrical concerns, call us instead of waiting for a form response.
