05 / Before you get in touch

A few useful answers.

Can you help with smaller electrical jobs?+

Yes. We handle outlets, lighting, troubleshooting, and repairs, alongside larger installation projects.

Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger?+

It depends on your existing electrical service and charging requirements. We'll assess your panel and planned charger before recommending any changes.

What should I include in my request?+

Your town, how to reach you, and a short description of the project or problem are a useful starting point. The questions above help you work through the details.

What happens after I send a request?+

We'll discuss what you need, what needs a closer look, and the next steps. This is a design preview, so the form on this page does not send a request.
