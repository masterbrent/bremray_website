Panel upgrades

You may need more power for new equipment, or you may have a problem with your existing panel. We'll check what you have and what you want to add before recommending an upgrade.

  • Replace damaged or unreliable equipment.
  • Check the electrical capacity before you add a charger, hot tub, or other equipment.
  • Find out why breakers keep tripping.
  • Review a panel concern from your insurer or home inspection.

A new panel doesn't always mean a larger electrical service. For some EV charging installations, equipment that manages the charging rate can avoid that extra work.

EV charger installation

We install home EV chargers, including Tesla Wall Connectors and other Level 2 chargers. Tell us which vehicle you drive, where you park, and whether you already have a charger.

We'll check the charger's requirements, the wiring route, and how much power your home can supply. Those details help us work out the installation and the price. We also install outlets for compatible plug-in chargers and handle permits for the work.

Read about home charging

Generators and backup power

Start with what you want to keep running when the power goes out. The furnace, refrigerator, sump pump, and air conditioning can need different amounts of power. We'll help you compare two options:

  • Portable generator connection: You bring the generator out and start it when you need it. We check the generator and your panel before choosing a compatible connection.
  • Automatic standby generator: The generator starts and supplies power automatically after an outage. We size the system around the equipment you want it to run.
