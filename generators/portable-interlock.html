Keep the essentials running

A portable generator connection lets you power selected circuits through your panel. You might want the refrigerator, furnace, sump pump, lights, and internet. We check what your generator can run together.

How an interlock works

The interlock physically prevents the utility main breaker and the generator breaker from turning on together. That keeps generator power from feeding back into utility lines.

The kit must fit the exact panel and installation. We check compatibility before recommending it. Some installations need a different transfer arrangement.

What we install

  • A compatible interlock and secured generator breaker
  • An outdoor inlet that matches the system
  • Wiring sized for the installation
  • The required labels, grounding, and bonding connections

We handle the electrical permit and inspection arrangements, test the setup, and show you how to use it.

Already have a generator?

Tell us the make and model. We check its voltage, running output, outlet, and neutral arrangement against the proposed connection. The size of the outlet alone doesn't tell us how much power it can supply.

Don't alter a generator's neutral connection yourself. The generator and transfer equipment need to work together as a system.

What can it run at the same time?

That depends on the generator's running capacity and the starting demand of each appliance. A 50-amp inlet doesn't guarantee enough power for everything in the house.

Start with the essentials

List the refrigerator, furnace, well pump, sump pump, lights, and other equipment you need. We'll check the actual loads.

Then consider larger appliances

Electric heat, water heaters, dryers, ranges, and air conditioning can use a large share of the available power. You may need to leave some off while the generator runs.

We explain which circuits you can use and how to manage them. You won't need to work that out during the first outage.

Choosing a fuel

We can supply suitable tri-fuel generators that run on gasoline, propane, or natural gas. Check the selected model's output on the fuel you plan to use; its highest advertised starting wattage isn't its continuous output.

A natural gas connection can avoid refilling tanks while the supply remains available. We coordinate that work with a gas contractor. The generator still needs maintenance and shutdowns according to its manual.

Safe setup matters

Never run a portable generator indoors

Keep it outside, at least 20 feet from the house, with exhaust pointing away from buildings, windows, doors, and vents. A garage or shed is not a safe running location, even with the doors open. Use working carbon monoxide alarms in the home.

Follow the written procedure for your installed equipment. Never connect a generator to an ordinary household outlet. Shut it down and let it cool before refueling.

We help you plan the running location and connection before installation. If the property can't accommodate safe placement, we need to consider another option.

Before the next outage

  • Keep the operating instructions with the equipment.
  • Follow the generator's maintenance and fuel-storage instructions.
  • Check the cord and connections for damage before use.
  • Make sure the person who will operate it understands the switchover procedure.
