Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

How We Handle Your Information

Bremray Electrical respects your privacy. Here's what we do with your information.

What We Collect

When you contact us, we collect:

  • Your name and phone number
  • Your email address (if you provide it)
  • Your address (for service calls)
  • Information about your electrical needs

How We Use It

We use your information to:

  • Contact you about your electrical service request
  • Schedule appointments
  • Send invoices and receipts
  • Follow up on completed work

Who We Share With

We don’t sell your information. We share information with the services and people who help us handle your request or carry out the work:

  • Housecall Pro (to receive service requests, follow up, and manage appointments)
  • Our accounting software (for invoicing)
  • City inspectors (when required for permits)
  • Google Analytics, only if you accept optional analytics cookies, as described below

Our website form sends your contact details, answers, and preferred way to reach you to our secure server, then to Housecall Pro so we can follow up about your job. We keep a private delivery record to help avoid duplicate or lost requests. Sending this form does not sign you up for marketing. If you choose the “Open the form in Housecall Pro” link, you’ll leave our site and use their hosted form. That form has its own privacy controls. See Housecall Pro’s privacy policy for details.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Ask us what information we have about you
  • Request we delete your information
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Update incorrect information

Website Cookies

Cloudflare helps protect our website from automated abuse. It may set a security cookie called __cf_bm when you visit, before you make an analytics choice. Cloudflare says this cookie expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. See Cloudflare’s cookie information.

We load Google Analytics only if you choose “Accept analytics.” It uses optional cookies to measure page visits, clicks on our telephone links, and completed service requests. A telephone-link click does not confirm that a call or service request was completed. We count a service request only after Housecall Pro confirms receipt. We do not send your name, contact details, answers, or form contents to Analytics, and we remove query strings and fragments from the page and referring URLs we send. Advertising signals and ad personalization are disabled.

You can reject analytics and still use this website, or change your choice with “Cookie choices” in the footer. Your preference is saved in this browser’s local storage. Rejecting after accepting stops further analytics collection and removes this site’s Google Analytics cookies; it does not delete data already collected. If browser storage is unavailable, we ask again on your next page visit.

Google processes analytics information according to its Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Security

We use encrypted website connections and restrict access to customer information. Contact us if you have a concern about information you sent through this website.
