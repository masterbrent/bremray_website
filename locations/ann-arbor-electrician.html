Electrical work for your home

Bremray is a licensed and insured electrical contractor based in Ann Arbor. We've served the area since 2004. Tell us what you want to add, replace, or repair. We'll help you work out what the job needs.

EV charger installation

Charge at home where you park. We'll check the charger, the route for the wiring, and the power available before we plan the installation.

Panel upgrades

Adding equipment or concerned about your panel? We'll look at what you have and explain whether you need a repair, a replacement, or more electrical capacity.

Backup generators

Tell us what you want to keep running during an outage. We'll help you compare an automatic standby generator with a connection for a portable generator.

Lights, outlets, and repairs

We add lights, ceiling fans, outlets, and circuits. We also find and repair problems when something stops working.
